Who we are

This policy applies to the African Brands Congress website (africanbrandscongress.org). It explains what personal data we collect when you register, send an enquiry or use the site, and what we do with it.

Data controller (legal name): To be announced

What we collect

We only ask for what we need. Please do not put sensitive details, such as health or financial information, in message fields.

  • Registration form: full name, email address, phone number, organisation, job title, country, sector and, if you choose to tell us, how you heard about ABC.
  • Contact form: name, email address, subject and your message.
  • Exhibitor, sponsor and award enquiry forms (when available on this site): name, email address, phone number, company, role or category of interest, and your message.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request details, recorded by our hosting provider. We use your IP address to run the site and to limit repeated or spam submissions.

Cookies and analytics: To be announced

How we use your data

  • To manage your registration and email you a confirmation with the date and venue.
  • To reply to your enquiries.
  • To send you Congress updates.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam.

Legal basis for processing: To be announced

Who we share it with

Your details are seen by the ABC team members who manage registrations and enquiries. We also use service providers who handle data on our behalf:

  • Vercel: hosts this website and processes form submissions.
  • Google (Google Sheets): stores the registration and enquiry records.
  • Brevo: sends the notification and confirmation emails.

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose it if the law requires us to.

Sharing with event partners, sponsors or exhibitors: To be announced

Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside Nigeria.

Safeguards for these transfers: To be announced

How long we keep it

Retention period: To be announced

How we protect it

  • Forms send your details over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Registration records are held in a Google Sheet that only named ABC staff can open.
  • Service credentials are kept in secure server settings, not in the website's code.

If a personal data breach puts your rights at risk, we will report it and tell you as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict, or object to, how we use it;
  • give you your data in a portable format; and
  • stop using it, if you gave us consent and now withdraw it.

To make a request, email us at the email address to be confirmed. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Children

This website is for business professionals and is not aimed at children.

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest update at the top.
