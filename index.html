What is ABC?

The African Brands Congress is an annual pan-African event that brings together brand owners, marketers, executives, regulators and investors to learn, network and celebrate African brand excellence.

Read more

  • Learn

    Keynotes, masterclasses and panels on how AI is reshaping brand building in Africa.

  • Network

    Structured networking with brand owners, executives, regulators and investors from across the continent.

  • Celebrate

    Recognising African brand excellence through the African Brand Leadership Merit Awards.

Sub-themes

Each track looks at a different part of how AI is changing the way brands are built and run.
