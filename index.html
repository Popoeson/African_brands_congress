What is ABC?

The African Brands Congress (ABC) is an annual pan-African event, running for over a decade, that brings together brand owners, marketers, executives, regulators and investors to learn, network and celebrate African brand excellence.

  • Learn

    Plenaries, masterclasses and panels.

  • Network

    Structured networking with brand owners, executives, regulators and investors.

  • Celebrate

    Recognition of outstanding African brands through the awards.
