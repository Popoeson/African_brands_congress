African Brand Leadership Merit Awards

Recognising excellence in African brand building across 35 categories.

Overview

The African Brand Leadership Merit Awards celebrate the brands, teams and leaders raising the standard of brand building on the continent. Winners are recognised at African Brands Congress 2026.
