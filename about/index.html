About the Congress

An annual pan-African gathering to learn, network and celebrate African brand excellence.

Who ABC is

The African Brands Congress (ABC) is an annual pan-African event for the people who build, grow and govern brands on the continent. Brand owners, marketers, executives, regulators and investors meet to learn from one another, make connections and celebrate the brands setting the standard.

Organiser background to be announced.

Mission and objectives

Our mission

To strengthen African brands by connecting the people who build them with the ideas, relationships and recognition they need to grow, at home and on the global stage.

Our objectives

  • Equip brand leaders with practical knowledge on adopting AI responsibly.
  • Connect brand owners with agencies, regulators and investors.
  • Showcase and reward African brand excellence.

Benefits of attending

  • Practical AI know-how

    Leave with frameworks and examples you can apply to your brand the following week.

  • Senior-level connections

    Meet the CEOs, brand leaders and investors who make decisions in African markets.

  • Cross-market perspective

    Hear how brands in different African markets are approaching the same challenges.

  • Policy and regulatory insight

    Understand where regulation of AI and marketing is heading, directly from regulators.

  • Recognition and visibility

    Celebrate the brands setting the standard through the merit awards.

  • Partnerships and deals

    Find agencies, suppliers and partners ready to help you grow.

Expected outcomes

What the Congress aims to leave behind once the day is over.

A published communique on AI adoption, ethics and policy

The Congress will publish its recommendations for brands, regulators and policymakers on adopting AI responsibly, shared with every attendee after the event.

  • Practical frameworks attendees can take back to their teams
  • New partnerships between brands, agencies and investors
  • Recognition of outstanding brands through the merit awards
  • A clearer view of the regulatory landscape for AI in marketing
