Exhibition and Sponsors

Put your brand in front of the people shaping Africa’s brand landscape.

Why exhibit or sponsor

African Brands Congress 2026 brings brand owners, CEOs, marketers, regulators and investors together for one focused day in Lagos. Exhibiting or sponsoring puts your organisation at the centre of that conversation.

Package details and availability are shared on enquiry.

Make an enquiry

What you get

  • Brand exposure

    Put your products and services in front of a focused, senior audience for a full day.

  • Access to senior decision-makers

    Meet the CEOs, brand owners and marketing heads who hold budgets.

  • Networking

    Join structured networking sessions and build relationships across markets.

  • Brand visibility

    Appear across Congress materials, signage and channels before, during and after the event.

Who should exhibit

  • Banking and financial services
  • Telecommunications
  • Technology and AI
  • Media and advertising
  • Creative and marketing agencies
  • Packaging and print
  • Retail and e-commerce
  • Manufacturing
  • Hospitality and tourism
  • Healthcare and pharmaceuticals
  • Energy
  • Logistics
  • Public sector and regulators
